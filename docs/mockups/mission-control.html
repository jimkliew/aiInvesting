breakout_20d_volscale, the quant book for season 4

Hypothesis (Idea Agent, season 4, round 1): a stock that closes above its previous 20-day high tends to keep rising for several weeks. Hold it while it stays above its 20-day average. Size each position by inverse volatility so the calmest names get the most capital, and cap any single name at 30%.

Universe traded: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, META, TSLA, AVGO, AMD, CRM, ORCL, NFLX. SPY is the benchmark only; the strategy never holds it.

Critic: Keep. Positive in 5 of 6 validation sub-periods. Turnover 140% per year is inside the 300% limit from lesson 7.

Indicators the code computes

generate_signals, exactly as the Coder Agent wrote it

def generate_signals(prices, features): # prices: date x ticker closes. features["ret_1d"]: daily returns. high20 = prices.rolling(20).max().shift(1) # yesterday's 20-day high sma20 = prices.rolling(20).mean() entry = prices > high20 # breakout above the range exit_ = prices < sma20 # trend lost held = entry.where(entry | exit_).ffill().fillna(False) vol20 = features["ret_1d"].rolling(20).std() * 252 ** 0.5 raw = held.astype(float) / vol20 # inverse-vol sizing w = raw.div(raw.sum(axis=1), axis=0).fillna(0.0) return w.clip(upper=0.30) # no name above 30%

How the engine turns weights into trades

equity = 1.0 for day in trading_days: w_target = weights.loc[day] # decided at yesterday's close w_held = positions.shift(1).loc[day] # one-bar lag: no same-day fills cost = COST_BPS / 1e4 * abs(w_target - w_held).sum() pnl = (w_held * returns.loc[day]).sum() - cost equity *= 1 + pnl positions.loc[day] = w_target

The one-bar lag is the simplest honest execution model: a signal computed at tonight's close can only be traded tomorrow. The cost model charges 5 basis points on every unit of weight that changes. Both are fixed for the campaign so seasons stay comparable.

Coach's reviews

After each season the Review Agent reads the scorecard, the track record, the changelog, the notebook and the desk's best and worst decisions. It may only recommend changes from the lever menu.

After season 3, 2025 Q3 team +9.5%, SPY +8.1%

Assessment. The team beat SPY by 1.4 points. Lesson 4 (re-enter after a drawdown reverses above the 20-day) fired three times and all three were profitable, adding about 1.1 points. Raising the desk's maximum weight (change 3) added 0.4 points of return and 18% turnover with no change in drawdown. Raising the critic's validation bar to 0.8 (change 5) left only one Keep candidate in research, so the selector reused last season's book.

What worked. Trend strategies in a trending quarter; the re-entry lesson.

What failed. The stricter critic starved the book of candidates.

revert(5) Put the critic's minimum validation Sharpe back to 0.5. One Keep in four ideas is too few to choose from; the bar was the cause, not the ideas.
Expect: 2 to 3 Keep candidates per round. Revert if: fewer than 2 next season.

trader_momentum.md

You are a momentum trader managing one sleeve of a long-only portfolio. Each week you receive a snapshot for one ticker: recent closes, where price sits against its 20, 50 and 200-day averages, RSI 14, returns over 1, 3 and 12 months, realised volatility and your current weight. Decide one action: BUY, SELL or HOLD, with a target weight between 0 and the maximum allowed, a confidence from 0 to 1, and one sentence of reasoning grounded in the numbers shown. Prefer names in established uptrends. Cut positions that lose their trend. Never reference information not in the snapshot. ## Lessons from the coach 1. (after S1) Do not call a stock oversold unless RSI 14 is below 30 and it sits below its 50-day average. Dip buying lost 1.6 points in 2025 Q1. 4. (after S2) After a drawdown larger than 8% reverses above the 20-day average for two weeks, re-enter rather than wait for the 50-day.
